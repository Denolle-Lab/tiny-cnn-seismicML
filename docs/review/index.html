tiny-cnn-seismicML · label review

Do you agree with these labels?

Each card is one stored 60 s window (2 to 20 Hz; 2 to 45 Hz for the aircraft stations; waveform and spectrogram) with the label a rule gave it. Every window starts as Agree; click Disagree on the ones that look wrong, add a note if you like, then download the CSV. Decisions are kept in this browser until you reset. Look for: Noise quiet with at most small transients; Traffic sustained 5 to 20 Hz energy (day) or one to two 3 to 6 s vehicle passes (night burst); Train a 20 to 40 s spindle with an emergent onset; Aircraft a 30 to 90 s broadband burst concentrated above 15 Hz, seen at both Turnagain stations. The verification report explains each rule.

Windows shown as plots only; waveform files are not published. Raspberry Shake data: "Data powered by Raspberry Shake, S.A., a citizen-science project. Please visit raspberryshake.org and join the Citizen Science Community today! DOI: 10.7914/SN/AM." AK data: Alaska Earthquake Center via IRIS, DOI 10.7914/SN/AK.

CSV columns: reviewer, reviewed_at, id, prefix, source_stamp, window_id, network, station, start_time_utc, local_time, label, label_name, rule, label_method, event_id, decision, note, rms, rms_band, band_ratio. Merge it into the metadata files with python scripts/apply_review.py review.csv.
